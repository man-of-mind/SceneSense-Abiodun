## 1. The locked calculation

For each of the 100 target pedestrian actor-frames:

```
visibility = area(B_visible) / area(B_full_clipped)
truncation = 1 - area(B_full_clipped) / area(B_full_unclipped)
```

`B_full_clipped` and `B_full_unclipped` are the axis-aligned projections of the
actor's eight oriented-bounding-box corners, clipped and unclipped to the
1280x720 frame. `B_visible` is the tight 2D box enclosing the depth pixels that
survive the actor-volume test below. Truncation is computed separately and never
folded into visibility.

### Coordinate transformations

World is CARLA's left-handed UE frame in metres. In the sensor frame `x` is
forward, `y` right, `z` up; the CARLA depth camera encodes planar `x`.

1. **Oriented box.** `corners_world = R_z(yaw) @ (+/-ex, +/-ey, +/-ez)^T + centre_world`,
   from the recorded `object_world_{x,y,z}` bbox centre, the recorded
   `gt_extent_{x,y,z}_m` half extents and the recorded `object_yaw_deg`, with
   `R_z(psi) = [[cos, -sin, 0], [sin, cos, 0], [0, 0, 1]]`.
2. **Projection.** `p_cam = camera_inverse @ [p_world, 1]`, then
   `u = cx + (y/x) * fx`, `v = cy - (z/x) * fy` over corners with `x > 0.05`.
3. **ROI.** The integer window `floor/ceil` of the clipped projected box, the
   same rasterisation rule the frozen collector uses.
4. **Back-projection.** For each valid depth pixel (finite, `0 < d < 1000 m`) at
   pixel centre `(u, v) = (col + 0.5, row + 0.5)`:
   `x = d`, `y = (u - cx) * d / fx`, `z = -(v - cy) * d / fy`, then
   `p_world = camera_matrix @ [x, y, z, 1]`.
5. **Actor-local.** `p_local = R_z(yaw)^T @ (p_world - centre_world)`.
6. **Containment.** Retain iff `|p_local| <= (ex, ey, ez) + 0.05 m` elementwise.
7. **Ground rejection.** Additionally require `p_local.z > -ez + 0.03 m`, so road
   and ground-contact pixels cannot become actor support.
8. **Competing pedestrians.** A point inside more than one pedestrian volume is
   awarded to the box with the smallest normalised actor-local distance
   `|| (lx/ex, ly/ey, lz/ez) ||_2`, ties broken by the lexicographically smallest
   actor id, so the rule is independent of candidate ordering.
9. **No support.** Zero retained points gives `visibility = 0` and `no_support = true`.

Depth decoding uses the registered CARLA BGRA decoder; run-time equality against
`data_collection/route_b_perception_v3/visibility_v1.decode_depth_bgra`
(sha256 `4a7aa974...`) is asserted on a random probe and recorded as
`decoder_bit_identical`.

### One area convention, fixed in advance

Retained pixels live on the integer grid while `B_full_clipped` is a continuous
rectangle, and the ROI is the *outward* rasterisation of that rectangle. A tight
pixel-extent box can therefore overhang the projected box by up to one pixel per
side — for a small distant pedestrian that is a large fraction of the box, and it
produced ratios up to 1.20 in synthetic testing. `B_visible` is consequently
defined as the tight pixel-extent box **intersected with** `B_full_clipped`, i.e.
a genuine sub-box. This is a fixed convention applied identically to every
sample, not a tuned constant, and it means the `[0, 1]` clamp only ever absorbs
floating-point round-off. The un-intersected ratio is still reported per sample
as `visible_box_raster_ratio`.

## 2. Source resolution

`dataset/manifest.csv` from the named camera-plane dataset supplies every camera
intrinsic and extrinsic. Its sibling `dataset/object_boxes.csv`, however, retains
only contract-`POSITIVE` rows and **omits 22 of the 100 pilot targets**, so it
cannot satisfy the "exact joins for all 100 samples" requirement.

Box geometry and the old depth-interval metric are therefore read from the frozen
validation view that the pilot itself registered by hash in
`sample_manifest.csv` (`source_manifest_sha256`, `source_object_boxes_sha256`,
`source_depth_diagnostics_sha256`); all three match byte-for-byte. Two
qualification checks pin this down:

* `dataset_and_view_calibration_identical` — all 11 calibration/path/frame/timestamp
  columns are identical across the two manifests for all 100 samples.
* `view_and_episode_box_geometry_identical` — all 12 box-geometry columns agree to
  a maximum absolute difference of exactly `0.0` against the raw per-episode
  `object_boxes.csv`.

The coverage gap is recorded, not papered over, in the
`dataset_object_boxes_coverage_reported` check.

## 5. Why it fails, from the overlays

The contact sheet shows the geometry doing exactly its job: retained points sit
on pedestrian bodies, and road and ground pixels that fall inside the actor's
global depth interval are no longer counted. The failure is one of **scale, not
of localisation**.

On the 28 samples a human called `bare` (essentially unoccluded), the automatic
score has median 0.763 and maximum 0.936; only 4 of 28 reach 0.90. The median
visible-box height ratio is 0.896 and width ratio 0.874 — the projected oriented
box is systematically larger than the pedestrian's rendered silhouette, because
it carries head/foot clearance and, once yawed, its axis-aligned projection is
wider than the body. A box-area ratio against that cuboid therefore saturates
near 0.76, while the Caltech/CityPersons band edges were written for a
*silhouette* visible fraction.

The consequence is a one-sided compression: of the 43 disagreements, 28 are
one-band under-estimates and 4 are two-band under-estimates, against 11
over-estimates. 20 of the 28 human-`bare` samples land in `partial`. The
`partial | bare` edge at 0.90 is the single largest contributor to the kappa
shortfall, while the `>= 0.65` decision — which does not depend on that edge —
reaches 0.8523 balanced accuracy.

A handful of disagreements are annotator variance rather than method error: for
example panel 008 has the pedestrian half behind a tree trunk (automatic 0.553,
`heavy`) yet is labelled `bare`, although the rubric counts vegetation as an
occluder.

| Median visibility monotonic across the four bands | strictly increasing | 0.2096, 0.3659, 0.7116, 0.7628 | PASS |
| Linear weighted Cohen's kappa | >= 0.60 | **0.4581** | **FAIL** |
| Balanced accuracy at >= 0.65 | >= 0.80 | 0.8523 | PASS |
| Not worse than old on kappa | >= 0.2043 | 0.4581 | PASS |
| Not worse than old on balanced accuracy | >= 0.5568 | 0.8523 | PASS |

Five of six gates pass and the method more than doubles the old metric's kappa
while lifting the `>= 0.65` decision from near-chance (0.5568) to 0.8523. The
registered kappa bar is not met, so:

**`ACTOR_VOLUME_VISIBILITY_PILOT_NOT_FEASIBLE_RETAIN_HUMAN_BANDS`**

No constant was retuned and no check was weakened to reach this. The human bands
remain the reference. Full validation-set rescoring of FCOS or LR-ASPP is not
authorised by this audit and requires a separate review.
