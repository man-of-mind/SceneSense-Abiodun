HOW TO READ
  • Tracked vehicle — confirmed by ≥2 confident detections in 3 frames. Drawn along its observed travel direction, or along the lane where the detector's accumulated heading agrees with it. Nose marks the producer-supported front when known.
  • Vehicle, orientation not established — rounded square: position is tracked but the heading evidence is too weak, or disagrees with the lane, to draw a direction.
  • Person — same rule, warm colour. A short tick shows walking direction once movement is statistically supported (no minimum speed).
  • Person, motion unresolved — faint ellipse: the detector's range error (long along the camera ray). Standing and slow walking cannot yet be told apart; the person stays shown.
  • Tentative — one confident detection, not yet confirmed.
  • Missed update — no new detection for >350 ms; fades out by 750 ms.
  • Low-confidence hypotheses (Inspect only) — recent sub-threshold detections, with labels and counts.
  • Overlapping detections — one dashed outline where two tracks claim the same space; which one is real is unresolved. Inspect shows each.
  • Shaded area — 2D estimate of what static buildings leave in camera line of sight. Not qualified visibility.
