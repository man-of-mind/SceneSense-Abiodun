HOW TO READ
  • Tracked object — confirmed by ≥2 confident detections in 3 frames. Nose shows travel direction when known.
  • Person — same rule, warm colour. A short tick shows walking direction once movement is statistically supported (no minimum speed).
  • Person, motion unresolved — faint ellipse: the detector's range error (long along the camera ray). Standing and slow walking cannot yet be told apart; the person stays shown.
  • Tentative — one confident detection, not yet confirmed.
  • Missed update — no new detection for >350 ms; fades out by 750 ms.
  • Unresolved hypotheses — recent low-confidence detections. Not vehicles; not counted as objects.
  • Conflicting pair — two tracks occupying the same footprint; at most one is real.
  • Shaded area — 2D estimate of what static buildings leave in camera line of sight. Not qualified visibility.
